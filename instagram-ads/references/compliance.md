# Meta Ads Policy — Compliance Reference

## Character Limits (Ad Copy)
| Field | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text | 125 chars | Text truncated after this; longer copy OK for storytelling but risks "more" cutoff |
| Headline | 40 chars | Shown below image/video |
| Description | 30 chars | Optional; shown below headline |
| Link display | 30 chars | Custom display URL |

## Text-on-Image Rule
Meta penalizes ads where more than 20% of the image is covered by text. Avoid heavy text
overlays. Use minimal, punchy text if any (5 words or fewer performs best).

---

## Restricted & Prohibited Content

### Prohibited (will be rejected)
- Tobacco, tobacco products, e-cigarettes
- Illegal drugs or drug paraphernalia
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives
- Unsafe supplements (steroids, ephedra, etc.)
- Adult content or nudity
- Sensationalist or shocking imagery
- Misleading claims ("guaranteed", "cure", false before/afters)
- Payday loans with APR > 600%

### Restricted (requires prior approval or special ad account)
- Alcohol
- Financial products (loans, crypto, trading)
- Gambling / gaming
- Pharmaceuticals / health supplements (claim-sensitive)
- Political / social issue ads (requires authorization)
- Dating apps
- Real estate (fair housing restrictions apply)
- Employment ads (special ad category)
- Credit ads (special ad category)

## Flagged Copy Phrases (Avoid These)

These phrases commonly trigger rejection or ad disapproval:

**Personal attributes** (Meta prohibits implying knowledge of personal characteristics)
- ❌ "Are you struggling with depression?"
- ❌ "If you have diabetes..."
- ❌ "For people with bad credit..."
- ✅ "For anyone dealing with stress..." (general language OK)
- ✅ "Designed for those managing their health..."

**Misleading claims**
- ❌ "Lose 10 pounds in 10 days" (specific, unverified medical/weight claim)
- ❌ "Guaranteed results"
- ❌ "Clinically proven" (without substantiation)
- ✅ "Thousands of customers love their results"
- ✅ "Designed to support your weight loss journey"

**Before/After imagery**
- ❌ Dramatic before/after photos (especially for weight loss, skin, body transformation)
- ✅ Single-state lifestyle imagery with testimonial text

**Urgency / Pressure**
- ❌ "Act NOW or lose access forever" (manipulative urgency)
- ✅ "Offer ends Sunday" (legitimate deadline is fine)

---

## Health & Wellness Specific Rules

This is a heavily scrutinized category. Key rules:
- Cannot claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease
- No specific weight loss numbers ("lose 20 lbs")
- Supplements cannot make drug-like claims
- No imagery that could be considered shocking or distressing
- Before/after photos heavily restricted for weight, skin, and body

**Safe language for health products:**
- "Supports", "promotes", "designed to help", "as part of a healthy lifestyle"

---

## Financial Products Specific Rules

- Must display APR, fees, and loan terms where required
- Cannot target by financial status
- "Get rich quick" language prohibited
- Crypto requires pre-approval in most regions
- Must comply with local financial regulations (country-specific)

---

## Appeals & Rejections

If an ad is rejected:
1. Read the rejection reason in Meta Ads Manager
2. Edit the specific element flagged (usually copy or image)
3. Re-submit — do not duplicate and re-run without edits (can lead to account flags)
4. If rejection seems incorrect, use the "Request Review" option

Repeated policy violations can lead to ad account restriction or permanent ban.
