# Instagram / Meta Ads — Targeting Playbooks

## Core Targeting Types

### Interest-Based (Cold Audiences)
Best for top-of-funnel. Stack 2–4 related interests per ad set. Keep ad sets between 500K–5M
audience size (for smaller budgets) or 1M–10M (for $100+/day spend).

**Do**: Narrow with demographics (age, gender) when the product is clearly demographic-specific.
**Don't**: Over-narrow — let Meta's algorithm find the buyers within a broader pool.

### Behavior-Based
- **Online shoppers**: "Engaged shoppers" behavior — Meta's best purchase intent signal
- **Business owners**: Job title + Small Business owner behavior
- **Travelers**: Frequent travelers, recent international travel

### Custom Audiences (Warm)
Upload or connect to build audiences from:
- Website visitors (pixel-based) — segment by page visited, time on site
- Video viewers — 25%, 50%, 75%, 95% completion tiers
- Instagram engagers — profile visitors, post engagers (30/60/90 days)
- Email list — upload CSV; use for retargeting or exclusions
- App activity (if applicable)

### Lookalike Audiences
Build from your best custom audiences:
- 1% LAL of purchasers = highest quality, smallest size
- 2–3% LAL = good balance of quality and reach
- 5–10% LAL = broad reach, lower precision

**Best seed audiences for LALs (in order of quality)**:
1. High-value purchasers (LTV top 25%)
2. All purchasers
3. Add-to-cart / initiated checkout
4. Email subscribers
5. Video viewers (95% completion)

## Niche Targeting Playbooks

### E-Commerce / DTC
**Cold**
- Interests: competitor brands, relevant publications, lifestyle interests
- Behaviors: Engaged shoppers + category interest
- Lookalike: 1–2% of purchasers

**Warm**
- Website visitors (last 30 days, exclude purchasers)
- Add-to-cart non-purchasers (last 14 days)
- Instagram engagers (last 60 days)

**Hot (Retargeting)**
- Initiated checkout, non-purchasers (last 7 days)
- Purchasers (upsell/cross-sell, last 180 days)

---

### Service Business / Local
**Cold**
- Radius targeting: 10–25 miles around location
- Demographics: age range matching customer base
- Interests: related to the service need

**Warm**
- Website visitors from service pages
- Instagram engagers

**Note**: For local businesses, keep budgets tight on radius — broader geo = wasted spend.

---

### B2B / SaaS
Meta is not as strong as LinkedIn for B2B, but can work with right targeting:
- Job title targeting (limited but available)
- Company size behaviors
- Interest: industry publications, tools (e.g., "HubSpot", "Salesforce")
- Lookalike from CRM upload

Use Meta for top-of-funnel brand awareness + retargeting warm leads from LinkedIn.

---

### Coaching / Info Products / Courses
**Cold**
- Interests: relevant thought leaders, books on topic, podcasts, competitor brands
- Behaviors: small business owners (if B2B), or lifestyle interests (if B2C)

**Warm**
- Webinar/lead magnet registrants
- Video viewers (50%+)
- Email list

**Key**: Lead with free value offer (lead magnet, webinar, free training) for cold audiences.
Hard selling to cold audiences rarely works in this niche.

---

## Exclusion Audiences (Always Apply)
Always exclude to avoid wasting spend:
- Recent purchasers (last 30–180 days, depending on product repurchase cycle)
- Existing customers when running acquisition campaigns
- People who've already seen the same ad 10+ times (use frequency caps)

---

## Audience Size Guidelines

| Budget/Day | Recommended Audience Size |
|---|---|
| <$50 | 200K – 1M |
| $50–$200 | 500K – 3M |
| $200–$1K | 1M – 10M |
| $1K+ | 5M+ or broad (let Meta optimize) |

**Broad targeting** (no interest/behavior stacking, just demographics) works well at $500+/day
budgets where Meta has enough data to self-optimize via Advantage+ audiences.
